About the project

Re:Order is a personal web-app project exploring a simpler way to record everyday care. Its design brief was informed by OCD-related concerns and the burden of managing detailed tracking interfaces.

Design priorities

Optional fields, neutral language, hide/show controls, and no streaks or reward scores. These are product decisions, not claims that the app reduces anxiety or treats OCD.

Development

The project creator defined the concept, feature priorities, interface direction, and revisions. Implementation and automated checks were assisted by OpenAI ChatGPT/Codex. Branding assets were adapted with AI from supplied references.

Current scope

The prototype includes sleep, food and drink, body notes, medication records, print views, and JSON backup. It stores records in browser local storage. It does not implement remote AI analysis, automatic cloud sync, or clinical assessment.

Limits

Browser records can be lost and are not encrypted by the app. Medication timestamps document user actions, not confirmed ingestion. No clinical validation, clinician endorsement, or measured health benefit is claimed.
