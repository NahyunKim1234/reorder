No app account · Records stored in your browser · Dark & light mode

01 / SLEEP

Your own rhythm.

Record sleep intervals, naps, and an optional note or dream.

02 / FOOD & DRINK

A few taps, a simple record.

Add a saved routine, count water or caffeine, and choose whether calories are shown.

03 / BODY

As much detail as you want.

Keep symptom notes or habit-urge counts. Basic vitals stay collapsed until needed.

04 / MEDICINE

A timestamped record.

Press and hold to record a reported dose. View inventory and optional care notes.

Your records, your copy.

Print a calendar overview or detailed timeline for a selected period. Download a JSON backup to keep a copy or move records.

Records are local to this browser, with no automatic cloud sync. Clearing browser data can remove them. Keep a backup if you need to retain your records.

A personal software prototype. For self-reported tracking, not diagnosis or treatment. Medication records reflect user input, not verified ingestion.
